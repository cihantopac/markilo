# Göçebe — design decisions


## 2026-11-01 — Explore: three directions (owner unavailable; Markilo's picks)

Form language from the brief: durable + grounded → heavy constant strokes, few parts, low base; free-spirited → one open gesture; warm → soft unequal radii. Embroidery-safe: details ≥ ~100/1024.

1. **stitch** (wordmark-led, letter): lowercase göçebe; the ç cedilla is a running stitch of thread. Hook at 32 px: a c whose tail is stitched. Stranger sees: "a c with a stitch" → mending. Indigo #263a5e + ochre #d39b38. Risk: stitch dashes vanish small; can read as a broken letter.
2. **webbing** (audience world, no letter, no object): one heavy strap that loops back and crosses itself over-under. Hook: an over-under crossing. Madder brown #6e2a1d + ochre. Risk: celtic knot / infinity / interlocking cliché.
3. **bohca** (object with a named transformation): a tied square bundle whose two knot ears are the dots of ö. Hook: a square sack with two ears. Olive-pine #2f4a3a + ochre. Risk: gift bag / money sack reading.
Evidence: research summary (11 sources, references Vaude/Deuter); palettes ΔE ≥ 18 from the danger red (color compare).

## 2026-11-01 — Gate round 1: stitch and bohca rejected

Critic blind averages: stitch 3.4 (reads "cp", ç no longer correct), bohca 4.4 (reads money-bag emoji); both present = no, next = reject. Webbing dropped at sketch review. Not reworked (per workflow); round 2 = new ideas:

4. **menderes** (place world, no letter/object): a heavy band with one deliberate meander turn — the Büyük Menderes near İzmir gave the word "meander". Hook: one bold turn of a wide band. Aegean teal #1f4e5a + copper #a8612b. Risk: S / Greek key / road sign.
5. **cay** (negative space): two cupped forms leave a tulip çay glass between them — the shared stop on the road. Hook: an hourglass/tulip of empty space between two heavy shapes. Madder brown #6e2a1d + amber #d39b38. Risk: vase / hourglass / Rubin vase cliché.
6. **percin** (wordmark-led): heavy grotesque göçebe; ö dots and ç cedilla are copper rivets, the only coloured parts. Hook: copper rivets in a dark word. Indigo-plum #3b3a5c + copper #a8612b. Risk: too quiet; icon (ö) can read as a face.
