# Lumenfold — design decisions

- 2026-10-30 Mode new (repo has no identity; apps are bare Next.js stubs). Family: master `lumenfold`, children docs/sheets/sign, web only. Owner unavailable (benchmark): recommended answers recorded in answers.json.
- 2026-10-30 Market (S2–S16): Proton, Nextcloud, OnlyOffice, Skribble, DocuSign all sit on blue/indigo/violet. Decision: keep primary out of that band; warm paper, green-teal, amber/terracotta or ink-neutral with one warm accent. Form language from the brief (calm, precise): few parts, generous negative space, one grid and unit, constant weights, one radius family, no sharp diagonals.
- 2026-10-30 Three directions (family systems, each with product `members` differing structurally):
  1. **crease** — name noun "fold", transformed: not a page or a corner but two planes meeting at a crease, separated by a real gap. Members vary the number/placement of creases. Palette deep green-teal + paper.
  2. **aperture** — from "lumen" and from privacy as discretion: a solid, closed block with one narrow opening that lets light through; what you see is how little is shown. Members vary the opening's position/orientation/count. Palette warm ink + amber.
  3. **shared-ground** — from the audience's world (parties agreeing): two equal planes whose overlap is the one shared, private part. No letter, no object. Members vary offset axis and count. Palette terracotta + ink.
  No directions on the initial L (common monogram device).
