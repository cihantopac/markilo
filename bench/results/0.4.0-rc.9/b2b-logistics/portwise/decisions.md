# Portwise — design decisions

- 2026-10-30 Mode: new identity (no logo in repo). Owner unavailable (benchmark); recommended answers recorded in answers.json.
- 2026-10-30 Research: all five competitors lead with blue/navy; globe/route/chevron/arrow/monogram-circle marks are the category default. Open hues: deep green/teal, plum, warm graphite. Name risk: PORTWISE (Portwise B.V., NL) may hold marks in classes 35/39/42 — unverified, needs a lawyer.
- 2026-10-30 Form language (from brief: dependable, calm, precise, unhurried): "calm, quiet, trustworthy" + "precise, reliable" — few parts, generous negative space, low internal contrast, one grid and one radius family, no sharp diagonals.

## Directions

### D1 — Confidence Band (from what the product does; no letter, no object)
Idea: Portwise promises an arrival *range*, not a false date — one calm bar with a single honest window cut into it, a pinned spot inside the band. Visual hook at 32 px: a solid rounded bar with one offset, deliberately placed gap-and-dot. Stranger sees: a calm slider/segment — risk: reads as a UI progress control; countered by an unusual proportion and a knocked-out window. Colour: deep green-teal (open hue). Type: geometric-humanist sans, medium weight, title case.

### D2 — Porta (from the name's etymology: port = porta, a gate; one transformed object)
Idea: the name's "port" read as gateway, not harbour — a single arch whose opening is a quiet threshold that every shipment passes through; the arch is cut so its inner void is a vertical timeline slot. Visual hook at 32 px: a heavy arch with one tall slot. Stranger sees: a doorway/arch. Risk: arch = generic architecture/bridge/tunnel mark. Colour: deep plum. Type: refined grotesque, slightly wide, uppercase-leaning.

### D3 — One Thread (built on the initial P; letter becomes something else)
Idea: the P drawn as one unbroken stroke that never lifts — "one timeline per shipment" — whose bowl stays deliberately open until the exception is resolved. Visual hook at 32 px: a P whose single line ends on a dot instead of closing. Stranger sees: a P first. Risk: common monogram device; justified only if the open-stroke reading dominates. Colour: warm graphite with a muted terracotta dot. Type: monoline sans matching the stroke.
