## Directions (Explore)

1. **Settled** (id `settled`) — audience's world, no letter, no object. Idea: the double underline an accountant draws beneath a final total, the gesture for "this is done" — two soft strokes, the lower shorter and offset. Visual hook at 32 px: two unequal rounded bars stacked, like a rest under a line of music. Stranger sees: a calm "underline / equals". Colour: deep plum + warm paper. Risk: reads as an equals sign or text-align icon; offset and length must make it a gesture.
2. **Runway** (id `runway`) — product mechanism (forecast), no letter, no object. Idea: one continuous line, solid and firm where money has landed, then handed on as an open, gently rising second stroke separated by a real gap — today and the next 90 days. Visual hook: a single stroke that breaks once and steps forward. Stranger sees: a path continuing. Colour: ink + ochre. Risk: reads as a chart line or a generic swoosh; keep it stepped/geometric and specific, not an arrow.
3. **Tally to line** (id `fanline`) — the name's literal noun, with a named transformation (the only literal/name-led direction). Idea: a tally becomes a line: upright ticks that lean progressively until the last lies flat, counting turning into a settled line. Visual hook: a fan of three–four rounded strokes sweeping from vertical to horizontal. Stranger sees: a fan/ticks falling to a baseline. Colour: clay-berry on paper. Risk: tally-mark trope and busy at 16 px; keep ≤ 4 strokes with wide gaps.

Not chosen: T/L monogram (initial-with-a-cut is a common weak device), page/coin/bar-chart marks (category clichés).
