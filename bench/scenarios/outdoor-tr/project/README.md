# Göçebe

Outdoor and travel gear designed in İzmir: packs, layers and camp kitchen gear built to be repaired, not replaced.
Every product has a ten-year repair promise. The shop ships across Türkiye and the EU.

## Products

- Travel packs (28 L, 40 L) and daypacks
- Merino and recycled-fleece layers
- Camp kitchen: stoves, pots, utensils

## Who it is for

Hikers and long-distance travellers, aged 25–45, in Türkiye, Germany and the Netherlands. The site is Turkish and
English.
