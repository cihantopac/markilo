# Dozeline

A wind-down and sleep journal app for iOS and Android.

Dozeline helps people who sleep badly build a calmer evening: a guided 20-minute wind-down, a two-question sleep
journal each morning, and gentle soundscapes. No wearables and no sleep scores — just patterns you can act on.

## Screens

- Wind-down: a guided 20-minute routine that dims the screen step by step
- Journal: two questions every morning, weekly patterns
- Sounds: rain, distant train, fan, brown noise

## Audience

Adults with irregular sleep (shift workers, new parents, students), English and Turkish at launch.
