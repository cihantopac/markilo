# Sunvale Energy Cooperative

Neighbours co-own solar panels on school, library and warehouse roofs. Members buy shares from €50, vote on new
projects and receive a share of the income; surplus funds energy advice for low-income households.

## Features

- Member portal and app: your shares, your production, your vote
- Live production of every roof
- Annual general meeting and online voting

## Who it is for

Households and small businesses in mid-sized towns; members are 20 to 85 years old.
