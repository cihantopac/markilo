# Quorvo

Meeting notes that write themselves, and the follow-ups nobody forgets.

Quorvo joins your calls (Zoom, Meet, Teams), writes a short summary with decisions and owners, and turns every
"I'll send that over" into a task in the tool your team already uses. Nothing is recorded without the host's consent;
transcripts are deleted after 30 days unless a team keeps them.

## Features

- Summaries with decisions, owners and open questions, in under a minute after the call
- Follow-ups pushed to Linear, Jira, Asana or Slack
- Search across every meeting the team kept
- Host consent and automatic deletion by default
- English, German, French and Spanish

## Who it is for

Remote and hybrid teams of 5–200 people: product, engineering, customer success.
