# Emberwick Coffee

A small roastery and café in a former printworks. We buy green coffee directly from eight farms, roast twice a week
in small batches and ship within 48 hours of roasting. Subscriptions let people choose roast style and rhythm.

## What we sell

- Single-origin and two house blends, whole bean or ground
- Subscriptions: every 1, 2 or 4 weeks
- Brewing gear and workshops at the café

## Who it is for

Home brewers and offices who want traceable coffee, in the UK and the EU.
