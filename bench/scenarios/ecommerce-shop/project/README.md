# Kilnroom

An online shop for small-batch ceramics from independent makers.

Every piece on Kilnroom comes from a studio of one to five people. Makers list each firing as a small "drop"; buyers
can follow makers, get notified when a kiln is opened, and see how each piece was made. Kilnroom handles payments,
insured shipping across Turkey and the EU, and breakage replacement.

## Features

- Drops: each firing goes on sale at a set time
- Follow makers and get notified when a new kiln opens
- Insured shipping with breakage replacement
- Maker profiles with process photos and studio notes
- Turkish and English storefront, prices in TRY and EUR

## Who it is for

People who buy handmade tableware and gifts, and the small ceramic studios that sell to them.
