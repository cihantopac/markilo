# Tallyline

Invoicing and cash-flow forecasting for freelancers and small agencies.

Tallyline turns time entries and fixed-fee projects into invoices, chases late payments politely, and shows a
90-day cash-flow forecast so a two-person studio knows whether it can afford next month.

## Features

- Invoices from time entries, milestones or retainers, in 30 currencies
- Automatic, polite payment reminders
- 90-day cash-flow forecast with "what if" scenarios
- Client portal with online card and bank payments
- Exports for accountants (CSV, DATEV, Xero)

## Who it is for

Freelancers, designers, developers and small agencies (1–20 people) who invoice clients directly.

## Stack

Next.js app router, Prisma, Stripe.
