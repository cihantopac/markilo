Inktrap

draft
checks 24/28 · 1 failinsufficient evidence · low

A heavy lowercase word cut like a type designer's proof: every join of m, a, r and k carries the same deep ink trap, and the k-i-l stems rise above the font's ascenders into one tall, even skyline.

  • the ink trap is a detail only people who draw type would make, so it says studio, not generator
  • one cut geometry (same depth, same funnel) repeated at eight joins reads as a system
  • the raised k-i-l stems give the word a silhouette no stock grotesque has (typeset likeness 0.74)
  • the trapped k, where three traps converge, stands alone as the icon
  • the icon is still a letter k, the weakest kind of icon, and its traps fill in below 32 px where it is just a heavy k
  • the traps disappear below about 24 px wordmark height, so small uses rely on the tall stems alone
  • the slits in the a and the lower k join can read as chips at mid sizes (48-96 px)
  • stem proportions are Schibsted's, so the face is recognisable to type people
Details & mockups

Ink traps are the type designer's own craft detail; exaggerated and made consistent, they show that Markilo is made by people who draw letters, not by a generator

Seal

verified
checks 16/16insufficient evidence · low

One knife angle makes the whole identity: a terracotta block whose top is cut at it, a k carved out standing on the block's edge, and every stem of the word cut top and foot at the same angle, with the i dotted by the block itself.

  • Warm, hand-tooled and clearly not a generator logo: nothing in the category looks pressed or carved
  • One consistent cut angle ties seal, word and i-dot into a single system that also works in one colour
  • The seal survives at 16 px as a distinctive slanted silhouette
  • The icon is still a letter in a shape: the k carries the name but not a product truth, and could be read as a generic k app icon
  • The chisel cuts on the word are quiet below ~40 px, where it reads as plain Alegreya Sans
  • The tilted top can read as a flag or a price tag at a glance
Details & mockups

Every identity begins with a mark of the maker; Markilo's own is a carved seal, warm and exact, the opposite of the category's bright, flat generator look.
