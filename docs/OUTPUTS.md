# Asset outputs

`markilo export --brand <slug>` writes everything below to `brand/brands/<slug>/exports/`, plus `index.html` (a
preview of every file on the background it is made for — open this first), `README.md` (the files most people need and
what each folder holds) and `asset-manifest.json` (purpose, pixel size, bytes, SHA-256 and source master of every
file). Numbers live in
[`src/export/specs.ts`](../src/export/specs.ts) with their official sources; they were last verified on 2026-10-30.

## `tokens.css` custom properties

All names are prefixed with the brand slug (`--<slug>-...`).

- Colour scales: `--<slug>-<scale>-<step>` (brand scales) and `--<slug>-neutral-<step>`, plus `--<slug>-ink` and
  `--<slug>-paper`.
- Ground colours: `--<slug>-on-dark` and `--<slug>-on-light` (`onDark`/`onLight` in `tokens.json`), only when the
  identity sets a different brand colour for dark or light grounds. Reversed logos, social images, the TV banner, the
  guidelines' reversed tile and dark-appearance app icons then use that colour instead of a computed lighter or darker
  shade (the dark favicon only where the primary is too faint on the dark browser bar); locked identities are never
  recoloured.
- Brand tile: app icons, avatars and the appmark put the symbol on a tile filled with `primary`, with the mark adapted
  to reach 3:1 on it. A palette `tile` (`concept update --tile`) changes the tile's ground, and `onBrand`
  (`--on-brand <#hex|auto>`) colours the whole mark in one colour there (`auto`: paper or ink, whichever contrasts
  more). The proposal previews use the same tile. Without either field every file is unchanged; locked identities
  ignore both.
- Semantic colours: `--<slug>-success`, `-warning`, `-danger`, `-info` and the other keys of `palette.json`'s
  `semantic`. Their hues are fixed on purpose, so a success or error colour stays recognisable whatever the brand
  colour is.
- Spacing: `--<slug>-space-<n>` for n = 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, in 4 px units (`--<slug>-space-4` is 16 px),
  the same steps as `tokens.json`.
- Font: `--<slug>-font-brand`, the brand typeface followed by `system-ui, sans-serif` (only when the brand has one).
- Scheme pair: `--<slug>-surface` and `--<slug>-text` hold the brand's own ground and a readable text colour. A
  `@media (prefers-color-scheme: ...)` block swaps them for the other scheme (`dark` for light-first brands, `light`
  for dark-first brands).

Markilo does not design a type scale or corner radii, so neither is generated: generic values would be noise rather
than part of the identity. Define them in your product's design system.

Copy assets into an app with `markilo install-assets --brand <slug> --group web --into public` (dry run) and add
`--apply` once you have reviewed the plan.
